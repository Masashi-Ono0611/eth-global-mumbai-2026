Duck walk

Night of 6 to 7 Nov. A walking policy trained in simulation for our duck: Waveshare ST3215-HS 12 V servos, about 2.5 kg, the heavier Microduck head, and no foot switches (they are not fitted). Trained on one rented RTX 4090, 150 M steps in 20 minutes, starting from the open-source Open Duck Mini v2 training code.

1. The trained walk (version A)

Forward, turn, forward while turning, side-step, backward, stop. The keys shown are the ones the real-duck runtime uses.

2. How it learned

Same duck model, same "walk forward" command, five points in training. The untrained policy outputs almost nothing, so it holds the standing pose; by 10 M steps it shuffles, by 43 M it walks.

The datasheet estimates (weight, servo numbers) matter little. Delay is the real risk: the Radxa's loop must read the 14 servos and the IMU without adding more than about 40 ms. The policy itself takes 0.7 ms on the Radxa.

5. Status on the real duck

  • The runtime is installed on the Radxa. The same code walks the simulated duck 96 cm.
  • Waiting for: assembly, the BNO055 on header pins 27/28 (I2C4), the servo adapter on the Radxa's USB, and the joint sign test.
  • Then, one step at a time: read-only check, IMU axis calibration, dry run, stand (hanging), walk (hanging), floor on a tether.

Everything above is simulation. Nothing has run on the real duck yet.
