ETHGlobal Mumbai 2026 · architecture brainstorm · working draft

Don't give a robot your wallet.
Give it a budget.

Owners give their robots USDC allowances, like "this duck may spend $10 a day". Each robot pays on its own inside that limit, asks a human when it goes over, and can be cut off with one tap. No gas, no chains, just dollars. The duck is robot #1.

ARC

The money and the rules

USDC on Arc mainnet. The owner's account (EIP-7702) enforces each robot's caps, expiry and revoke on-chain. Gas is paid in USDC by our relayer.

WORLD

The human check

Over the limit or a restricted item, the owner approves on their phone (World Agent Pass). Deny or expiry → the payment can't happen.

0G

The brain

Each purchase decision ("right item? sane price? pay, ask, or walk away") is a 0G inference call, logged with its request id as evidence.

CLOUDFLARE

The coordinator

Workers and Durable Objects relay, watch the chain and show the dashboard. Cloudflare can never authorise a payment.

The one rule: Arc is the authority, Cloudflare coordinates. If our servers vanish, no robot can spend more than its on-chain allowance.

System

The robot runtime is the same on every robot; only the "body" plugs in. We build everything with a simulated robot and a web gate first, then plug in the duck.

Flows

1 · Pay inside the budget 
sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  participant G as Gate
  participant R as Robot
  participant CF as Cloudflare

  participant A as Arc
  G->>CF: POST /invoices
  CF-->>G: invoice id → QR on screen
  R->>G: scans QR (untrusted input)
  R->>CF: GET /invoices/:id (amount, payTo)
  R->>CF: decide? (frame + invoice)
  CF->>OG: inference
  OG-->>CF: "pay" + request_id
  CF-->>R: pay (evidence logged)
  R->>CF: signed one-time payment
  CF->>A: relayer submits (gas in USDC)
  A-->>A: 7702 account checks caps ✓
  CF->>A: ChainWatcher sees the transfer
  CF-->>G: signed "paid"
  G->>G: open 🔓
2 · Needs a human (over the limit or restricted), approve and deny 
sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  participant R as Robot
  participant CF as Cloudflare (Approval DO)
  participant W as World IdP
  participant O as Owner phone
  participant A as Arc
  R->>CF: payment needs approval
  CF->>W: start device flow
  CF-->>O: "Duck wants $8 for chai, approve?"

  alt approved
    W-->>CF: signed ID token ✓
    CF->>CF: approver co-signs this exact payment
    CF->>A: submit (robot sig + co-sig)
    A-->>A: caps + co-sig ✓ → paid
  else denied / expired / outage
    W-->>CF: no token
    CF-->>R: denied: nothing signed, nothing paid
  end
3 · Offline robot 
sequenceDiagram
  participant R as Robot (no network)
  participant G as Gate / another robot
  participant A as Arc
  R->>G: signed one-time payment (via QR / BLE)
  Note over G: holds it until online
  G->>A: relays later
  alt budget still available
    A-->>G: paid ✓
  else cap already used
    A-->>G: reverts: first come, first served
  end
4 · Pair a robot · revoke · safe-stop 
sequenceDiagram
  participant R as Robot
  participant O as Owner
  participant A as Arc
  participant CF as Cloudflare
  R->>R: generate session key (never leaves device)
  R-->>O: pairing QR (address + nonce)
  O->>A: delegate: $10/day · max $5 each · until Sunday
  R->>CF: connect, signed with its key
  O->>A: revoke → next payment fails
  O->>CF: safe-stop → robot stops moving and signing
