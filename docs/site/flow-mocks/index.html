Flow mocks for the "Now, live" screen

Four proof-of-concept treatments of the same screen on /pitch/. Each shows how the technologies are used in one payment: the chai within budget, the thali that asks, and the revoke. Same background, same title; only the diagram differs. Open each one full-screen and let it loop. Leave notes with the comment button.

A. One payment, step by step

The rail of six stops stays. A light travels it and a caption appears at each stop saying what happens there; the loop alternates the chai (World is skipped: within the cap), the thali (0G asks, World approves, Arc settles the co-signed payment) and the revoke (Arc refuses the next one, in red). Closest to what is there now.

B. Three layers

A diagram in three bands: on devices you hold (your passkey, the robot's chip), on public networks (Arc: money and rules; 0G: brain and memory; World: a human's yes) and at the shop (the gate). Packets travel between them along labelled curves: grant, decide, signed payment, paid, then ask, yes with World ID, approval. The most architectural one.

C. The live receipt

Three receipts fill in line by line with the icons: invoice, 0G's decision, the chip's signature, Arc's settlement (a real transaction hash), the gate opening; the thali adds you and World ID; the revoke ends in a refused line. Concrete and demo-like rather than a diagram.

D. Orbit

The robot in the centre; You, 0G, World, Arc and the gate around it. Pulses run along the spokes in order while a caption explains each hop. Visually the most distinct, and it puts the robot where the story puts it.

All four say "On Arc mainnet, with real USDC" in the title block. Wording in the diagrams is a draft; the numbers follow the demo story (3.00 a day, 1.00 a go, chai 0.10, thali 2.00).
