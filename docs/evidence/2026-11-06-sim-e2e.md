## What failed and what was fixed

1. The deployed sim never booted (07fafa5). Hugging Face's CloudFront answers 404 with no CORS headers to any request
   whose `Referer` is a `*.workers.dev` page, so the MJCF, kinematics, meshes and policies all failed (`PHYSICS MJCF
   FAILED`, `boot failed`). The earlier hand check had run from a local origin. Fix c20b67b: `<meta name="referrer"
   content="same-origin">` in `apps/sim/index.html` (CORS requests still send Origin, which HF echoes).
2. The duck could not face a stall it reached from the side (first mismatch attempt, intent
   `d6ixrkgk64lt4lld2vpxzjqsze`, 0 USDC): after the thali it walked to the chai stall, arrived beside the counter facing
   sideways, the camera never saw the QR, and 0G looped `look`/`scan_qr` until `no finish within 14 turns`. Measured: below
   ~0.15 m/s forward the walking policy behaves as if standing, and a pure turn in place stalls after ~0.2 rad in either
   direction at random (12 of 14 trials), until the 90 s walk timeout, after which `walkTo` reported "arrived". With
   vx ≥ 0.15 the same turn is an arc that finishes in 2 to 5 s (8 of 8). Fix faed778: a 0.15 m/s forward floor on
   programmatic commands, a 12 s cap on the face turn, and a line approach (each stall gets a line-up point 0.7 m back;
   the run-in pure-pursues the approach line and arrives facing the screen). Checked twice over entrance → chai → thali
   → chai → samosa → gate → chai: every leg in 13 to 26 s, within 0.08 m (gate 0.14 m), heading error ≤ 0.24 rad, and each
   stall's own invoice decoded every time.
3. Driver: the funding transfer reverted (run A, [0xa691c2ea…0acf](https://explorer.arc.io/tx/0xa691c2ea39a03137bab19e377c7679a8ce18dba1efead79cf3c8a97349780acf),
   status 0, 21,000 gas used). `sendUsdc` (`packages/chain/src/Fund.ts`, scripts only) signs a fixed 21,000-gas transfer;
   the driver sent it after the grant had deployed the owner's account, whose code runs on a value transfer, so it ran out
   of gas. Run A's chai therefore failed on Arc (`ERC20: transfer amount exceeds balance`) after a correct 0G `pay` and
   chip signature. The driver now funds right after signup, while the account is counterfactual (as `wallet-browser.ts`
   does), and fails on `success: false` (run B).

Unfunded rehearsals before the fixes (no USDC moved) also exercised the loop on 07fafa5 with the referrer tag injected
by the driver: chai reached 0G `pay` → chip signed → Arc refused for balance; thali reached `ask_human` → denied.

## Seen, not fixed here

- Conversation order (wallet UI, `RobotIntentView`/`RobotConsoleView`, owned by the redesign session): all of an
  intent's nav steps render as one block before the errand's own bubbles, so "Walking to the gate" (after payment) sits
  above "What I saw at the counter" and "0G decided: buy it", and the duck's closing `say` sits above the errand's
  `walk_away` (`sim-08`, `sim-13`). Should interleave by time (turn `createdAt`, decision `created_at`).
- After a `walk_away` the nav brain rescanned once (`scan_qr` again; the Worker answered from the same errand, nothing new
  started) although its prompt says never to rescan a walked-away item.
- `sendUsdc` with its fixed 21,000 gas cannot fund a deployed account; anything that funds an existing owner needs gas
  estimation.
