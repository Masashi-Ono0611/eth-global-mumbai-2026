| World | Agent Pass only (World IdP OIDC, chain-agnostic). A payment over the per-payment cap or to a payee off the allowlist needs the owner's approval in World App, through the browser flow with `nonce` = the payment's hash. The backend verifies the token (`iss`, `aud`, `exp`, `nonce`, `auth_time`, `acr` Orb, `jti` once, `sub` = owner) and co-signs that one payment. Deny or expiry means nothing is signed and settlement reverts. On-chain token verification is the stretch (D15: it would publish the owner's World pseudonym, which the pitch says). An approval is a human decision, never an "age" check. IDKit and HumanRegistry are dropped. World's wallet can't replace ours (Safe on World Chain only). In the 6 Nov rehearsal the real Orb sign-in carried `amr` `mfa`, so `amr` is recorded, not judged (95a92bd), and World requires S256 PKCE (2b04027). Built in M6 (6 Nov 06:21 IST): endpoints are discovered from `auth.world.org` at runtime, and `auth_time ≥ approval created_at − 60 s` (there is no `opened_at` column, so this is up to 300 s looser than §9, accepted for the event) | prize text; S5b prototype; research 6 Nov; World.test.ts 10 cases |
